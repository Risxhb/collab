# Ability icon generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; transparent_background=true. Generated October 21, 2026. One call per icon, no reference images or third-party icon assets.

Art brief: [issue #43](https://github.com/Risxhb/collab/issues/43) including its Arcanist addition; palette and icon grammar from the supplied Last Bell art guide sections 5 and 13.5.

Packaging uses Pillow to trim fully transparent margins, proportionally downsample each glyph into a 194 px box, and center it on a transparent 256 px canvas (at least 31 px padding). Generated alpha is preserved. No glyph repainting or background removal is performed. Contact sheets composite copies on dark/light backgrounds and convert preview copies to grayscale.
