First arena is a staged increment, not a claim that all content exists. Shop transactions, upgrades/relics, progression and healing are deferred. No guard/parry, attack resources or invulnerability presets in production acceptance. Old two-class/two-boss/12-relic/six-modifier gate is retired.

Jumping does not imply rotating camera/open-world platforming or universal immunity. Hazards must explicitly declare any jump-clearance rule. Foundation owns shared project/packages/contracts; Codex owns arena delivery/integration, Claude primarily authors arena/character/environment content and viewer. Separate geometry/rules, Unity visual review, profiling, Windows build and human acceptance evidence.
