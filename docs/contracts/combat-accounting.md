# Combat accounting and death recap — PF-06 / PF-07

October 21, 2026; U3 0.1 proposal, no production binding. Scope: damage done/taken, immediate healing, shield absorption, segments and recap. Source review: #42 `18e89ed`, `Combat/CombatWorld.cs`, `Combat/PlayerController.cs`, `Combat/ChimesingerKit.cs`, `Combat/ArcanistKit.cs`, `RunDirector.cs`, `RunHud.cs`. Human meter scope remains open; this is the minimum coherent data specification, not Details! parity.

## One authoritative record

Every resolved damage/heal/absorb record has schema version; SessionId, RunId, AttemptId, SegmentId; monotonically allocated EventId and tick; AttackInstanceId, action request/cast ID where relevant; source actor and owner player IDs; semantic AbilityId and optional variant; target actor/player IDs; kind; requested amount; mitigation amount; shield absorbed; effective HP change; overkill/overheal; HP before/after; fatal/downed flags; optional parent/source event (Echo/expiry); world context and geometry resolution reference. Shield absorption is carried by the damage record to avoid counting the same absorption twice; shield grants/expiry have state-change records with zero HP change. Pet/projectile/Echo damage credits its owner; source actor still identifies the projectile or originating actor. Environment uses an explicit environment source, never an arbitrary player's ID. Health refills between fights are LifecycleRefill, not combat healing.

Deduplication key is **SessionId + AttemptId + EventId**, independent of display names, tick or ability labels. The separate attack-hit key (attempt + attack instance + target) prevents duplicate authoritative damage before events exist. A multi-target heal emits a different EventId per target. Replays preserve original IDs. Never sum a damage aggregate and its child components twice: either one aggregate hit or uniquely identified child hits; parent context records have no numeric contribution. A Convergence consuming two seals must document whether it emits one aggregate or two children and a zero-value parent; proposal: one hit per target with child seal IDs in provenance.

Damage arithmetic: `postMitigation = max(0, requested - mitigated)`; `absorbed <= postMitigation`; `available = postMitigation - absorbed`; `effective = min(available, hpBefore)`; `overkill = available - effective`; `hpAfter = hpBefore - effective`. Integer nonnegative HP units, with mitigation rounded once at authority (nearest integer, midpoint away from zero). Healing: `effective = min(requested, maxHp-hpBefore)`; `overheal=requested-effective`; `hpAfter=hpBefore+effective`. A damage record is fatal exactly when `hpBefore > 0 && hpAfter == 0`; downed versus terminal death is lifecycle state. A zero-HP-loss shielded hit is still a hit. Separate prevented/absorbed metrics; do not call them healing.

## Time, aggregation and retention

- Open a segment at the first combat-enabled completed tick of an encounter. It ends after the tick producing clear/defeat/practice stop/session termination. Store eligible combat-tick count; paused/intermission/preparation/result time contributes zero. Freeze duration at end; inspecting a results screen must not lower DPS.
- Segment DPS = sum effective outgoing HP damage / (eligible combat ticks / 60). A zero-duration segment displays 0 or “—”, never infinity. One tick is at least 1/60 s. Downtime and downed time during an ongoing encounter count; “active DPS” is deferred to avoid inflated comparisons.
- Run DPS = run effective damage divided by the sum of that run's combat segment durations, not wall-clock time from menu. Practice has its own group. Fresh retry creates a new run group; comparison history survives separately rather than silently blending attempts.
- Group rows by owner player + semantic ability; variants are optional subrows. Report effective damage, share of selected owner's/party total (label denominator), hit count including zero effective shielded hits, largest effective hit, DPS. Damage taken groups by target and source/ability. Healing reports effective, overheal and HPS over the same combat intervals. Absorption reports a separate amount. No support-score/critical/proc attribution invented without events.
- Minimum proposed history: current segment + last 20 completed segments per local session, last 5 run summaries; oldest completed entries evicted first. Keep totals/counts and a “detail truncated” flag when raw event retention is exhausted. Raw detail cap proposal 10,000 events per segment; preserve the fatal recap independently. These are bounded UI/storage defaults for review, not selected wire replay limits.
- Death recap: last six incoming damage records for the downed target in the current segment, with tick-relative timestamps, ability/source, effective/absorbed/mitigated, HP after and authoritative fatal flag. Include relevant heals/absorbs in expandable detail, distinguish them from the six incoming-hit rows. Freeze on down; later segments cannot contaminate it. A party-wide list's final event does not identify every player's killing blow.

The midpoint-away-from-zero mitigation rounding above is a proposed shared-policy choice, not parity with Unity's `Mathf.RoundToInt` midpoint-to-even rule. Review and fixture this difference before migration.

Event history has separate transport retention and acknowledgment watermarks. Receiving a snapshot sets state; receiving events fills feedback/accounting only once. Full accounting totals/checkpoint and replay watermark restore atomically. When detail is unavailable, show the gap/truncation and trusted aggregate totals; never silently fabricate a complete timeline.
